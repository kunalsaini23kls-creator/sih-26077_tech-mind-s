Project Overview

AI-driven hyper-local early warning system for secure weather nowcasting is an advanced, AI-powered meteorology and flood-prediction framework designed to provide real-time, location-specific weather insights and early warnings for Jaipur[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span). The system integrates live data feeds from the IMD Doppler Weather Radar (DWR), INSAT-3DS convective satellite imagery, and ground sensors to analyze rainfall, predict extreme convective cells, and help citizens and authorities take timely proactive action[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

Our goal is to mitigate the catastrophic impact of severe weather events like localized cloudbursts, flash floods, and thunderstorms across Jaipur by delivering accurate 0–180 minute forecasts, interactive GIS visualizations, and automated alerts — right when they are needed most[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
